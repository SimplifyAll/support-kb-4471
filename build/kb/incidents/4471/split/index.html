The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date like 6/5 (6 May) comes out as 5 June. Reports come from the app, email and the booking-v2 forms of all eleven tenants. This is the bug #4471 names. Fixed 5 Oct (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales).

hint: the booking-v3 form shows MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants fall here. The parser fix leaves this bug in place. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports can come from it.

one-day: the booking moves by one day. The month does not flip. The desk counts these reports under parser, where their tenant files them, so they show up in both rows. “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” “Every booking in June is one day off.” The desk already noted these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes). The pattern points at a time zone or daylight-saving conversion, not at date order. Neither the parser fix nor retiring booking-v3 touches them. Still open.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports with the same subject, and the same few subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”) cover all three problems. Its consequences only mention the opposite case: one bug split across different subjects. They don’t cover one subject holding several bugs. When a big ticket’s reports split by tenant, channel or form, read the bodies before you mark it known.
